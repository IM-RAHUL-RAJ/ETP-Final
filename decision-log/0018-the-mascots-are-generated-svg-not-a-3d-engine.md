# 0018 The mascots are generated, shaded SVG rather than a 3D engine

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | accepted |
| Date | 2026-10-07 |
| Decided by | Sprint 10 UI enhancement work (drafted by Claude from the sprint brief; the team to confirm) |

## Context

The brief asks for three cute animated 3D companions (owl, rabbit, cat) shown on empty states, in Settings, in the top bar and in e-mails, with idle, happy, thinking and loading animations.

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| SVG generated by one pure function, shaded with gradients and a contact shadow, animated with CSS | About 5 kB of code; sharp at any size; the same source renders the e-mail PNGs; respects reduced motion; no runtime dependency | Not real 3D (no rotation in depth) |
| Three.js models | Real 3D | Hundreds of kB for a decoration, a WebGL context per mascot, models to author and license, and nothing renders in e-mail |
| Lottie | Rich animation | Needs authored files, and a runtime of about 60 kB |

## Decision

`core/mascot/mascot-svg.ts` draws the three mascots in five poses. `<app-mascot>` animates them with CSS (breathing, blinking, a bounce, a turning ring while loading) and shows a thought on tap. `scripts/render-mascots.mjs` renders the same artwork to PNG for e-mails, because most mail clients show no SVG.

## Consequences

If the team commissions real models, `<app-mascot>` keeps its inputs and only the artwork source changes. The choice is stored per customer (`customer_preferences.mascot`).
