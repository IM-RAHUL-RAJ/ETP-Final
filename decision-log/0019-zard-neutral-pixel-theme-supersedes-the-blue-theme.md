## Context

The first pass used a blue accent, the Doto font only for headlines, and shaded vector mascots. The request was to move wholly to the Zard theme and the reference site, to make everything pixel-art in the Nothing dot font, and to have the mascot present at every step.

## Decision

- All text, charts and mascots are pixel styled. Doto is self-hosted (no Google Fonts request).
- Mascots are procedurally composed pixel sprites with stepped frame animations (idle, happy, dance, wave, cheer, think, sad), rendered as SVG in the app and as animated GIFs for e-mail.
- The e-mail mascot is embedded inline (`cid:mascot`) because a link to our own host is unreachable from a mail client.
- Notices are spoken by the mascot dock; every pop-up has a mascot.

## Consequences

- One palette in `styles.css`; blue tokens are gone. Old PNG mascot assets are unused.
- The GIFs are generated by `npm run` script `scripts/render-mascots.mjs` and committed under `services/trade-api/src/main/resources/email/mascots/`.
- The 3D engine remains out of scope (see 0018).
